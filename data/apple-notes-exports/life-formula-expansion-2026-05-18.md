## Your Strongest New Formula

```text
Luck Volume = Courage * Luck Surface Area

Luck Surface Area = Right Place + Right Time + Right People + Right Idea

Luck Volume =
  Courage *
  (Right Place + Right Time + Right People + Right Idea)
```

Why it works:

- `Right place` is geographic/contextual exposure.
- `Right time` is timing and technological/social wave.
- `Right people` is network density and trust.
- `Right idea` is prepared cognition meeting opportunity.
- `Courage` is the multiplier because opportunities do not matter if you do not act.

Refinement:

```text
Luck Captured = Courage * Luck Surface Area * Preparedness
```

Preparedness deserves its own term because luck can appear and still be missed.

## Open Theoretical Issue

Some terms should be additive, and some should be multiplicative.

Use additive formulas when components can compensate for one another:

```text
Project Fit = Money + Learning + Connection + Adventure + Fame
```

Use multiplicative formulas when any missing component destroys the result:

```text
Wealth Creation = Specific Knowledge * Accountability * Leverage * Judgment
```

This matters because it changes the practical advice. Additive formulas ask "which term can I improve?" Multiplicative formulas ask "which term is near zero?"
