## Better Top-Level Structure For The Essay

```text
Human Flourishing =
  Truth
  + Health
  + Wealth
  + Relationships
  + Purpose
  + Agency
```

Why this is stronger than only `Happiness = Health + Wealth + Good Relationships`:

- Truth keeps perception aligned with reality.
- Health keeps the body and brain usable.
- Wealth buys freedom and removes constraints.
- Relationships create belonging and emotional stability.
- Purpose chooses what all the freedom is for.
- Agency converts values into action.

```text
Agency = Clear Desire + Correct Model + Action + Feedback
```

Source logic: combines Naval's desire/self-awareness notes, OODA loop, and the repeated action/iteration material.

Source logic: combines Naval's "rederive from basics", judgment/leverage, and non-attachment material.

## Open Questions

- Whether `Purpose` should be a component of happiness or a parent concept above happiness.
- Whether `Truth` belongs inside `Wisdom`, `Agency`, or the top-level flourishing equation.
- Whether `Relationships` should split into `Family + Friends + Romantic Partnership + Community`.
- Whether `Good Relationships` should be treated as additive or multiplicative, because poor relationships can destroy health, wealth, and happiness.
- Whether `Wealth = Income + Wealth * ROI` should be rewritten more precisely as `Future Wealth = Current Wealth * (1 + ROI) + Savings`.
